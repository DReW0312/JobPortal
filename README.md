# Job Portal Platform 💼

A comprehensive career hub designed to bridge the gap between talent and companies. This platform streamlines the hiring process by offering dynamic job searching, one-click applications, and specialized dashboards for both users and HR teams to seamlessly oversee the entire recruitment lifecycle.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Dynamic Job Searching:** Advanced filtering and search capabilities to help candidates find the perfect role based on location, industry, experience, and keywords.
* **One-Click Applications:** A frictionless application process allowing users to apply for jobs instantly using their saved profiles and resumes.
* **Candidate Dashboard:** A personalized space for job seekers to track application statuses, manage their resumes, and save job postings.
* **HR & Recruiter Dashboard:** A dedicated portal for employers to post new job openings, review applicant profiles, and manage the interview pipeline.
* **Recruitment Lifecycle Management:** Tools to move candidates through different stages (e.g., Applied, Shortlisted, Interviewing, Hired).
